Brief History of the Prince Albert Mosque

The Muslim community of Prince Albert, Saskatchewan, has a rich history shaped by the contributions of several dedicated physicians. Among the early pioneers were Dr. Shakoor (ophthalmologist), Dr. Anwar Ahmad, Dr. Zahanat Khan, and Dr. Mateen, Dr. Rashid Mizanur (family physician and current board member) all played an important role in establishing the first mosque for the community.
The earliest gatherings for prayer took place in the basement of Dr. Rozina’s house, serving as the community’s initial place of worship. Later, Dr. M. Z. Hussain spearheaded the establishment of the present mosque building in 2009. Initially, the mosque was smaller, but it gradually developed into the current Prince Albert Masjid, which continues to serve as the center of Muslim life in the city.
