Brief History of the Prince Albert Mosque

The Muslim community of Prince Albert, Saskatchewan, has a rich and inspiring history shaped by the dedicated contributions of several physicians and community leaders. Our mosque stands as a testament to the faith, perseverance, and unity of our growing community.

Our Founding Pioneers

Among the early pioneers of our community were Dr. Shakoor, an ophthalmologist, and other dedicated individuals who recognized the importance of establishing a place of worship for the Muslim community in Prince Albert. These visionary leaders laid the foundation for what would become our vibrant mosque.

Early Gatherings

The earliest gatherings for prayer took place in the basement of Dr. Rozina's house, which served as the community's initial and most humble place of worship. These gatherings were characterized by strong bonds of brotherhood and dedication to our faith, despite the modest surroundings.

Establishment of the Mosque

The formal establishment of the mosque was spearheaded by Dr. M. Z. Hussain, whose vision and leadership transformed the community's aspirations into reality. Dr. Hussain moved to Prince Albert in 1971 with his devoted wife, and together they have remained steadfast supporters of our mosque throughout the decades.

To this day, the mosque continues to benefit from the unwavering support of the Hussain family, with Dr. Hussain's wife maintaining an active and honored role in supporting the community's spiritual and social endeavors.

Community Growth

Community involvement has been central to our mosque's continuous growth and development. Among the early contributors was Mr. Ismail, father of Brother Sammy Ismail, whose generosity and commitment set an example for others.

Our mosque has been enriched by the participation of people from diverse backgrounds, including communities from Central Africa, Somalia, and many other regions around the world. This diversity has strengthened our community bonds and enriched our collective experience.

Today and Beyond

In the present day, the mosque's day-to-day affairs are supported by dedicated leaders including Dr. S. Khan, an ophthalmologist whose clinic is located beside the mosque, and active community members such as Brother Sammy Ismail. Their commitment ensures that our mosque continues to serve the spiritual and community needs of all Muslims in Prince Albert.

Our Mission

The Prince Albert Islamic Association remains committed to:

  • Providing a welcoming space for prayer and spiritual growth
  • Fostering community bonds and mutual support
  • Serving the broader Prince Albert community
  • Preserving Islamic traditions and values
  • Supporting education and social welfare initiatives
